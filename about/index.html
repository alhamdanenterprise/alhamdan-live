Why Al Hamdan

Al Hamdan Enterprises was founded with a vision to create innovative businesses that provide real value to people while contributing to Pakistan's economic growth. The idea was not only to build successful companies but also to create opportunities, develop strong brands, and bring modern solutions to different industries. The journey started with a passion for entrepreneurship, technology, and long-term business development.

Vision

Over the next 5 to 10 years, the goal is to transform Al Hamdan Enterprises into a diversified and internationally recognized business group. Through brands such as Infiqo and Uroojj Fragrance, the vision is to expand into global markets, build strong customer-focused brands, embrace innovation and technology, and create sustainable growth that generates employment opportunities and positive impact for society.

"Pakistan has everything it needs to produce world class brands. We are here to prove it."
